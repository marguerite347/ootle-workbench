## Curation and testing scope

The owner approved this simpler policy on October 10, 2026. Lobby and Workbench resource listings are curated discovery. Routine maintenance checks descriptions against primary sources, real availability, creator/original-post links, accurately labeled recordings, and our own product UI. It does not require a full functional or security test of every third-party project before listing it.

- Card fronts explain purpose, resource type and useful availability: Web app, Desktop app, SDK, Source code, Concept, Testnet or Paused, as supported by evidence. Omit redundant labels. Keep material limitations visible when they affect the offered action: testnet-only, directly observed paused/unavailable service, relevant platform requirements or irreversible deposits. Put negative discovery findings such as no demo link found into dated Details rather than an undated absolute card-front claim, following the existing-listing re-review rules below.
- Do not display generic internal review wording such as Runtime not verified, Runtime untested, Not tested here, Claim untested, Hosting unverified or similar audit boilerplate on card fronts or catalog introductions. Keep useful detailed testing history/source qualifications in Details or the maintenance ledger. Removing a disclaimer must never imply a successful test, security audit or blanket Verified badge.
- Retain genuine screen recordings for every newly added resource, with accurate Docs walkthrough, Concept overview, Announcement or App demo captions. Documentation/source footage satisfies a source or concept listing's recording requirement; it does not demonstrate application functionality. Full capture method, date and limitations remain available in Details.
- Deeper functional testing is required when we actually integrate or depend on a resource, or explicitly claim to have tested a feature. Define and exercise that specific use, preserve dated version/environment/steps/results evidence, and recheck relevant changes. Adding an SDK or tool to the catalog alone does not create an integration or a runtime-testing obligation. Do not start a wholesale third-party certification pipeline.
- Apply this policy to existing and future entries in both products. Use existing content validation/publication checks to catch generic internal review wording in card-front fields while allowing useful detailed evidence. Continue source/link/media and our own UI checks, tool/dependency maintenance, original-post preservation, and desktop/mobile release verification.
